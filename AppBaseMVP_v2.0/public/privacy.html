Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. "KhataBase" ("the app") is operated by KhataBase Team. Contact: ganeshtekne1@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your khatabooks and entries are private to your account. Other users cannot see them, and the app has no sharing feature.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can export your data, and delete your account and all data yourself from inside the app.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a unique user ID (and, if you use email sign-in, a password that Google Firebase stores in hashed form; we never see it). If you sign in with Google we receive your email address from Google.
  • Your khatabooks: the names you give your books and their entries (amount, date, gave/got, note, payment mode), plus the list of payment modes you create.
  • Technical logs: Google Firebase / Google Cloud may log technical data such as IP address and device/browser information for security and operation.

How it is protected

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and travels over HTTPS. Security rules allow only the signed-in, email-verified owner of an account to read or write that account's data. Anyone operating the app (including us) should not access your entries other than for support you request or where the law requires.

Who processes your data

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting). Google processes account and ledger data on our behalf under its own terms. We do not send receipt images to Google Cloud or any OCR/AI provider.

Receipt scanning

If you choose to scan a bill, Tesseract.js and its English/Hindi language data run locally in your browser to read the selected image. The image is not uploaded or saved by the scanner. The OCR engine and language files are served from this app's hosting and may be downloaded the first time you scan. Review all extracted details before saving an entry.

On your device

To work offline, the app keeps a copy of your data in your browser's storage (IndexedDB). It also saves the last selected khatabook on that device in local storage, separately for each account. Signing out erases the offline database copy. On a shared or public device always sign out. The app does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and loads no third-party fonts or trackers.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data until you delete it. Use Menu → Delete account & data to permanently erase all your khatabooks, entries, payment modes and your account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Residual copies in Google's backups may persist for a limited period under Google's retention practices.

Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app, export it as CSV, and delete it as above. For other requests, email ganeshtekne1@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
